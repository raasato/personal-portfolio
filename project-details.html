Designing a More Connected Community

My Better Community: Better World Project: Using UX/UI Design and Web Development to Address Community Challenges.

Identifying a Community Challenge

Communication and access to information are importanat parts of maintaining a connected community. Within a condominium, residents, property management, and employees often need to share information, coordinate services, and stay informed about community activities.

However, when information and services are spread across different communication channels, accessing them can become inconvinient.

For my Better Community: Better World Proejct, I wanted to explore how a centralized digital platform could help address these challenges.

My project, Designing a More Connected Community, focused on designing and beginning the development of a condominium mangement website for Royal Capitol Plaza in Honolulu Hawai'i.

Understanding the Community

Before designing the website, I focused on understanding the people who might use it.

The platform was intended to support several stakeholder groups, including condominium residents, property mangement, maintenance employees, board members, and prospective residnets.

Each group has different responsibilities, expectations, and information needs.

To better understand these perspectives, I gathered stakeholder feedback and developed user personas, journey maps, and user stories.

These research activities helped me identify opportunities to improve communication, organize community resources, and create clearer pathways to resident services.

One of my most important lessons during this stage was recognizing that effective design begins with understanding the people who will use the product

Turning Research Into Design

After identifying stakeholder needs, I began exploring how the website could be organized and designed.

I used Figma to develop wireframes and high-fidelity prototypes that represented the platform's proposed interface.

My design process focused on creating clear navigation, organizing information logically, and considering accessiblity for users with different levels of technological experience.

I also evaluated feedback to identify opportunities for improvement.

Some of the issues I identified involved navigation labels, information organization, and features that were not immediately easy to locate.

This process reinforced the importance of evaluating designs rather than assuming that an interface will be intuitive to everyone.

From Prototype to Website Development

One of the most rewarding parts of my project was moving beyond the design stage and beginning to develop the actual website.

Using my front-end web development skills, I translated elements of my Figma designs into a basic website.

This allowed me to apply what I had learned about website structure, visual deisgn, and interface development.

Although the website is still in its early stages, completing its initial front-end foundation was an important accomplishment.

Future development will focus on implementing additional functionality, such as user accounts, maintenance request submissions, announcements, and dashboards for different stakeholder groups.

Challenges and Leadership Lessons

Throughout the project, I encountered challenges that helped me develop both technical and leadership skills.

One of the most significant challenges was balancing feedback from different stakeholders. Residents, property mangement, and employees did not always have the same priorities.

I learned that leadership involves listening carefully, evaluating different perspectives, and making decisions that support the community as a whole.

Another challenge was managing the project's scope while continuing to develop my technical abilities.

I needed to prioritize tasks, adapt my plans, and recognize which features would require additional development.

These experiences strengthened my problem-solving, communication, planning, and decision-making skills.

Project Outcomes

By the end of the project, I had completed several importnat deliverables:

  • Stakeholder research and analysis
  • User personas, journey maps, and user stories
  • Wireframes and high-fidelity Figma prototypes
  • Design evaluation and revisions
  • A basic front-end condominium website

While the project has not yet demonstrated long-term community impact through real-world platform use, it established a foundation for a digital resource that could help improve communication and access to information.

It also allowed me to apply my academic knowledge to a community-focused challenge.

Looking Ahead

My next goal is to continue developing the condominium management platform by adding back-end functionality, improving the user experience, and conducting additional usability testing.

I also want to continue building my skills in UX/UI design, web development, and accessible digital experiences.

This project reinforced my interest in creating websites and applications that serve a meaningful purpose.

My biggest takeaway is that technology can support positive commiunity change when it is developed with empathy, collaboration, and a clear understanding of user needs.

Through this expeirence, I learned that meaningful progress involves more than completing a tehcnical project. It also involves listening, adapting, reflecting, and continuing to impove.

Charity: Water 6KM Challenge

Using web development and interactive gameplay to raise awareness of the global water crisis.

Project Type: Interactive Web Development/Game Design

Program: Global Career Accelerator

Technologies Used: HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Organization: Charity: Water

GitHub Repository: https://github.com/raasato/6km-water-challenge

Project Overview

The 6KM Water Challenge is an interactive web-based game that I created as part of the Global Career Accelerator program.

For this project, the program introduced us to Charity: Water, a nonprofit organization working to address the global water crisis. Our challenge was to develop an original project that could help promote awareness of the issue and support the organization's mission.

I decided to create an interactive game that would allow users to experience a simulated version of the long journeys some people must make to collect water.

Rather than developing a traditional informational website, I wanted to explore how interactive gameplay could communicate a real-world challenge in a more engaging and participatory way.

The game centers on a 6-kilometer journey and incorpartes player movement, distance tracking, resource management, environmental challenges, and progress milestones.

Through this project, I had the opportunity to combine creative problem-solving with my growing web development skills while exploring how technology can be used to promote awareness of a global issue.

The Problem & Project Goals

Access to clean water is a global challenge that affects communities around the world. For some individuals and families, collecting water requires traveling long distances, making access to an everday necessity a phically demanding task.

While researching the issue, I became interested in how an interactive experience could help users better understand the effort involved in collecting water.

I wanted to create something that would encourage users to particpate in a simulated journey rather than simply read information about the global water cirisi.

This idea became the foundation of the 6KM Water Challenge.

My primary goals was to create an interactive web-based game that could raise awareness of the challenges associated with accessing clean water.

I established five goals to guide the project:

  • Raise Awareness: Introduce users to the challenges surrounding access to clean water.
  • Create an interactive experience: Allow players to particpate in a simulated journey toward a 6-kilometer goal.
  • Encourage Empathy: Use gameplay to help users consider the physical effort and obstacles associated with traveling long distances to collect water.
  • Apply Web Development Skills: Develop an interactive experience using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • Connect Technology with Social Impact: Explore how web development and game design can communicate improtant global issues.

These goals helped me establish a direction for the game and guided my decisions throughout the design and development process.

Research & Planning

Before developing the game, I researched Charity: Water and its mission to help provide clean and safe drinking water to communities around the world.

Through the organization's website, I learned more about the global water crisis and how limited access to clean water can affect people's health, education, and everyday lives.

One aspect of the issue that particularly influeced my project was the long distances some poeple must travel to collect water.

I wanted to translate this information into an interactive experience that would help users think about the time, distance, and effort involved in accessing water.

Developing the Game Concept

My research inspired me to develop a game centered on completing a simulated 6-kilometer journey.

I wanted players to experience a sense of progression while encountering challenges that could affect their journey.

To create this experience, I planned several gameplay features, including distances tracking, resource mangement, environmental condiditons, and progress milestones.

These elements were intended to make the experience engaging while reinforcing the game's connection to the global water crisis.

Planning the User Experience

When planning the game, I considered how users would interact with the different features and understand their progress.

I wanted the gameplay to be approachable while still incorporating challenges that required player participation.

I also considered how progress indicators, feedback, and changing environmental condidtions could help maintain engagement throughout the experience.

This planning stage helped me recognize how game mechanics and user experience design can work together to support a project's purpose.

Design & Development

After establishing the game concept, I began planning the interface and translating my ideas into a working website.

Early Wireframes

I started by creating simple wireframes to explore the game's layout and organization.

These initial designs helped me consider where interactive elements, progress indicators, and other gameplay information would appear on the screen.

Building the Game

I developed the 6KM Water Challenge using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

HTML provided the structure of the website, while CSS allowed me to create the game's visual appearance and organize its interface.

JavaScript was especially important because it allowed me to implement the interactive gameplay mechanics.

My focus was on creating a playable experience in which different features workied together as players progressed through the simulated journey.

Developing interactive Features

The game incorporates several interactive compoenets.

  • Player Movement: Allows players to progres through the journey.
  • Distance Tracking: Displays progress toward the 6-kilometer goal.
  • Resource Management: Introduces gameplay considerations related to water and the journey.
  • Environmental Challenges: Adds changing condidtions that affect the gameplay experience.
  • Progress milestones: Provides feedback as players advance.
  • Random Events: Introduces variation in gameplay through features that activate at different moments.

These features helped transform the project from a static website into an interactive game.

Developing them also gave me practical experience with JavaScript functions, conditional logic, event handling, and managing interactions between different componenets.

Refining the Development Process

As development progressed, I continued testing the game and making adjustments to improve its functionality.

I also worked on organizing the JavaScript code into clearer sections to make it easier to understand and maintain.

This stage helped me recognize the importance of code organization, testing, and debugging when developing interactive applications.

Challenges & Solutions

One of the biggest challenges I encournterd was getting the different game components to work together during gameplay.

Although individual features could function on their own, integrating them into a complete experience was more complicated than I initially expected.

The game included multiple interactive elements that needed to respond correctly to player actions, progress, and changing gameplay condidtions.

Coordinating Gameplay Events

One of the most challenging aspects of development was managing when certain features would activate.

Some events needed to occur at specific moments during the player's journey, while others were designed to happen randomly.

I needed to make sure these features activated under the correct condidtions without interfering with other gameplay components.

This required me to think carefully about how different JavaScript functions interacted and how changes in one part of the game could affect another.

Debugging and Testing

Debugging became a significant part of my development process.

I spent considerable time identifying why certain features were not behaving as intended, testing possible solutions, and adjusting the code.

Sometimes, resolving one issue requried me to examine how that change affected other parts of the game.

Through repeated testing and debugging, I gradually improved how the different features worked together.

Although this process was challenging, it helped me become more comfortable indentifying technical problems and working through them systematically.

Lessons From the Challenges

This experience taught me that developing an interactive application involves more than creating individual features.

It also requires understanding how different components interact, managing the timing of events, and testing the application as a complete system.

I strengthened my JavaScript debugging and problem-solving skills and learned the importance of patience and persistence throughout development.

What initially felt like one of the most difficult parts of the project ultimately became one of its most valuable learning experinces.

Project Outcomes & Reflection

By the end of the project, I had developed a playable web-based game that incorporated multiple interactive features.

The completed 6KM Water Challenge allowed me to apply my HTML, CSS, and JavaScript skills while exploring how an interactive experience could promote awareness of a real-world issue.

What I Accomplished

One of my biggest accomplishments was successfully integraing the different gameplay components.

Although the game has a relatively simple visual design, developing its functionality required significant time, testing, and problem-solving.

I am particularly proud that I was able to create a playable experience with several interactive features rather than focusing only on the appearance of the website.

Seeing the different compoenents work together during gameplay was one of the most rewarding parts of the project.

This experience showed me that the technical complexity of the project is not always reflected in its visual appearance.

Skills I Developed

Throughout the project, I strengthened several technical skills, including:

  • HTML and CSS development
  • JavaScript programming
  • Interactive game mechanics
  • Event handling and timing
  • Conditional logic and random events
  • Debugging and testing
  • Code organization and problem-solving

Beyond technical development, I also gained experience translating an orignial concept into a functioning digital experience.

The project challenged me to think creatively about how technology could communicate an important global issue.

Personal Reflection

Looking back, I am most proud of successfully developing the game's functionality.

While I recognize that the visual deisgn could be improved, I learned that creating an interactive experience involves much more than designing an appealing interface.

The most challenging and rewarding part was developing the logic that allowed the different gameplay features to work together.

This experience helped me become more confident in my ability to solve programming problems and develope interactive web applications.

It also reinforced my interest in JavaScript, creative coding, and interactive web development.

Future Imporvement

Although I completed a playable version of the 6KM Water Challenge, I recognize several opportunities for future development.

One area I would like to improve is the game's visual design. While I prioritized functionality during the initial development process, I would like to explore ways to make the interface more polished and visually engaging.

I would also like to continue refining the gameplay experience by improving how users interact with different features and making the game more accessible.

Another opportutnity is strengthening the connection between the gameplay and the educational purpose of the project.

For example, I could explore incorporating addtitional information about the global water crisis, clearer educational messages, or interactive elements that help users connect their gameplay experience to the real-world challenges the game represents.

These improvements would allow me to continue practicing my web development skills while expanding the game's potential as an awareness-buidling experience.

Conclusion

The 6KM Water Challenge was an important learning experience that allowed me to combine creative problem-solving, web development, and social impact.

What began as an open-ended challenge through the Global Career Acceslerator program became an opportunity to develop an original interactive game inspired by Charity: Water's mission.

Throughout the project, I gained experience planning an interface, developing JavaScript functionality, integrating interactive components, and debugging technical challenges.

Although the game is visually simple, I am proud of the fucntionality I was able to develop and the progress I made as a web developer.

Most importantly, this project helped me recognize how interactive technology can be used for more than entertainment.

It reinforced my interest in creating digital experiences that engage users, communicate meaningful ideas, and encourage contiued learning.
